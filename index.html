Data Preprocessing & Storytelling Summary

Raw enterprise datasets are inherently chaotic—filled with incomplete entries, string formatting errors, extreme statistical outliers, and duplicated transactions. Uncleaned data severely skews financial analytics, customer segmentation, and predictive models.

1. Deduplication & Standardization

Removed redundant transactions, trimmed whitespace, and unified heterogeneous date strings (e.g. 05/12/2024, May 12, 2024) to ISO YYYY-MM-DD format.

2. Robust Missingness Imputation

Replaced missing income and purchase metrics using category-wise medians to preserve true statistical distributions without introducing mean-bias.

3. IQR Outlier Boundary Capping

Identified extreme non-sensical values (e.g., ages of 170+ or $999,999 purchases) using Interquartile Range (IQR) limits and capped them to upper/lower 1.5×IQR limits.

4. RFM Customer Segmentation

Engineered Recency, Frequency, and Monetary (RFM) metrics to group customers into 5 actionable segments: Champions, Loyal Customers, Recent, At Risk, and Needs Attention.
